# Checklist 4: Product Vision
**Channel:** All Channels

**Goal:** Team agreement on the long-term vision and 18-month schedule.

**Reference:** [Chapter 4, Value Proposition Hypotheses/Product Vision](#)

- [x] What’s your long-term vision for your company?
		Elite level coaching for everyone.
- [x] What do you ultimately want to change or solve?
		Help people achieve movement they never thought was possible.
- [x] Are you going to do it with a series of products?
	 Yes.
		 1. a hardware/software solution for more easily video recording oneself
		 2. a social network for helping people connect with people at their favourite community place
		 3. a way for coaches to digitally monitor progress, evaluate form, provide feedback and create training plans
- [x] How do you expand into adjacent markets?
	 Other sports: Core technology will be used with minor modifications.
- [x] Do you need to get people to change their behavior?
	 Minimal behaviour change.  Eg/ Scan & Play 
- [x] What will the world look like 3 years after you arrive on the scene? Five years?
	 Athletes will have vast digital libraries of themselves performing their favourite sport.
	 They will be able to self identify problem movements/behaviours and take corrective action.
	 They will get elite level coaching suggestions on how to improve that they would not have had access to prior.
