# Checklist 28: Create a High Fidelity MVP
*Channel: Web/Mobile*

**Goal:** Develop a polished, functional MVP

**Reference:** Chapter 9, Customer Validation Phase 1: Get Ready to Sell

- [ ] Refined, more “complete” or polished version of the low-fidelity MVP used in Customer Discover to elicit customer feedback
- [ ] Part of the product – incomplete but polished rendering of product vision
- [ ] Limited features, but not second-class
- [ ] Invites more users to the product to test the acquisition and activation tactics
- [ ] Architected to deliver customer and product behavior data

**Exit criteria:**
- [ ] A High Fidelity MVP, which will be used to generate a steady stream of customer and product behavior data to refine the business model and product
