
The Business Model Canvas is a strategic management tool with **nine building blocks**, grouped into four areas: **customers, offer, infrastructure, and financial viability**.

---

## 1. Customer Segments

Defines the different groups of people or organizations a business serves.

Target
- climbing gyms
- climbers

More Broad
- indoor facilities providing athletics + athletes
		OR
- facilities or people with use for robotic video recording capabilities with app

---

## 2. Value Propositions

Describes the bundle of products and services that create value for a specific customer segment.

**Questions to consider:**
- What problems are you solving?
	- how can I improve at climbing?
- What needs are you satisfying?
	- easier video self recording
	- receiving Beta/coaching from others
	- community building

---

## 3. Channels

Outlines how the business delivers value to its customer segments.

**How**
Physical space in climbing gyms.
- advertising posters
- digital wall display
- QR code tags on problems

Social Media
- instagram?

**What**
- app
- hardware (camera, displays)


---

## 4. Customer Relationships

Describes the type of relationship a company establishes with each customer segment.

- Climbers = through app (tracking usage); in person at first
- Climbing gyms = personal
- Climbing coaches = personal

---

## 5. Revenue Streams

Represents the cash a company generates from each customer segment.

Gym subscriptions ($100 / month)

---

## 6. Key Resources

Lists the most important assets required to make the business model work.

- app
- video recording hardware
- print + lamination

---

## 7. Key Activities

Describes the most important things a business must do to deliver its value proposition.

- provides an app and hardware that makes it easier to do video self recording in climbing gyms more easily and with better video than any alternative
- people share beta and build community through app
- coaches grow their customer base using app


---

## 8. Key Partnerships

Identifies the external companies, suppliers, or partners that help the business succeed.

- climbing gyms
- coaches
- group leaders


---

## 9. Cost Structure

Outlines all the costs involved in operating the business model.

Premium for gyms and cost driven for climbers

**Approaches:**
- Cost-driven (lean, low-cost)
- Value-driven (premium, high-value)
